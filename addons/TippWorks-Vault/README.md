# Tippenhauer Works — Weekly Vault (90)

The level 90 Season 2 weekly gearing checklist, plus today's Bountiful Delves, as two tabs.

**Weekly tab.** These rows are read from the game:
- Great Vault reward
- Coffer Key Shards x/600 (and keys)
- Coiled Isle rares today, marked "not needed" once your shards are capped (needs TippWorks-Rares)
- Vault delves (Tier 8+)
- Manaflux charges and tier pieces
- Orin's weekly Nebulous Voidcore

You tick the rest yourself:
- keyed Bountiful clears
- the weekly bounty map
- Tier 11 Gilded Stashes

Hover any row for the details from the gearing guide. The checklist resets itself at the weekly reset.

**Bountiful tab.** Today's Bountiful Delves are read live from the zone maps, with the version
of each and the closest flight master. Click one for a waypoint, a share with your group and
directions, just like a Delver's Calls row.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/tw vault` | Show or hide the window |
| `/tw weekly` | Open the Weekly tab |
| `/tw bountiful` | Open the Bountiful tab |
| `/tw vault stash` | Show where the game reports the Gilded Stash count (for bug reports) |

In the AddOns menu, click opens the Weekly tab and right-click opens Bountiful.

Requires **TippWorks-Core**. Replaces the Weekly and Bountiful tabs of CoiledRareTracker; this week's
checklist is copied over the first time it loads.
