# Tippenhauer Works — Delver's Calls (80 → 90)

Levelling from 80 to 90 through delves. Each Midnight delve has a one-time **Delver's Call** quest,
which you pick up from an object near the entrance (Atal'Aman's is inside the delve). The window
lists all ten by zone, with the closest flight master, and each row shows the **next step**:

1. **pick up**: clicking sets a waypoint to the quest object.
2. **run delve**: clicking sets a waypoint to the entrance. Tier 1 is enough.
3. **done – hold to 87**: keep the finished quest until level 87.
4. **turn in!**: clicking tracks the quest so Blizzard's arrow leads to the turn-in NPC.

Clicking a row also shares the spot with your group and prints directions: how to get to the zone
from Silvermoon, which flight master to use, and what to look for. If you're standing in
Silvermoon, the waypoint goes to the zone's portal first. Click again once you're through.

- **Shift-click** a row to put the spot in your chat box instead of sending it.
- **Right-click** a row to mark it done or not done yourself.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/tw calls` | Show or hide the list |
| `/tw calls list` | Print every Call's status in chat |
| `/tw share group` / `say` / `guild` / `self` | Where a row click shares the spot (shared with Vault's Bountiful tab) |
| `/tw portal <zone>` | Standing on a portal in Silvermoon, save its spot for all your characters |
| `/tw map` | Re-find the zone maps if a waypoint fails |

You can also open the list from the Tippenhauer Works minimap button or the AddOns menu.

Requires **TippWorks-Core**. Replaces the Delves tab of CoiledRareTracker; your progress is copied
over the first time it loads.
